The DAPA Framework

Traditional benchmarks rely on Word Error Rate (WER), which is blind to the aural difference between correct and incorrect vowels in Arabic. We solved this by creating the Linguistic Fidelity Score (LFS).

1

Tashkeel Preservation Index (TPI)

Ensures every diacritic marker is phonetically represented in the audio duration and pitch shift.

2

Spectral Continuity

Measures the transition between Urdu and Arabic. A spectral variance ΔS > 1.5 indicates a robotic jump.

3

LFS Integration

A weighted composite prioritizing linguistic correctness over mere 'smoothness'.

The DAPA Equation
LFS = (0.4 · TPI) + (0.3 · CSacc) + (0.3 · Nat)

Technical Rejection Log

Why certain models failed the rigorous stress-test.

❌ EGTTS-V0.1

Dropout: 18.18%

XTTS v2 architecture struggles with the specific cadence of Arabic-to-Urdu switches. The high MCD indicates a failure in mel-spectrogram reconstruction of high-frequency diacritic markers.

❌ ArTST

Dropout: 38.41%

SpeechT5 baseline fails to parse complex tashkeel, leading to robotic and incorrect vowelization in sacred texts.

❌ MMS-TTS-Ara

Dropout: 17.04%

VITS architecture ignores nuanced tashkeel in the Hadith sections, resulting in poor intelligibility.
