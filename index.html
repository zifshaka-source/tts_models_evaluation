Our Unique Approach: DAPA

Unlike other repositories that rely on subjective "naturalness" scores, we implemented Diacritic-Aware Phoneme Alignment (DAPA).

We analyze the audio at the frame level to ensure that every Fatha, Kasra, and Damma is phonetically represented. If a model skips a diacritic, it is flagged as a Tashkeel Dropout, regardless of how "natural" the voice sounds.

LFS = w1(TPI) + w2(CSacc) - w3(MCD)
