The Evaluation Pipeline

A transparent, rigorous, and reproducible process for finalizing model verdicts.

1

Stress-Test Curation

Standard datasets fail because they are "too easy". We developed a 40-passage stress-test specifically designed to break neural attention mechanisms.

Tashkeel Density: Passages containing high-frequency 'fathah', 'kasrah', and 'damma' markers.

Ligature Stress: Using complex Urdu Nastaliq word-joins to test tokenization.

Switching Frequency: Transitions between languages every 5-10 words to ensure prosodic continuity.

2

Phonemic Alignment

We utilize a window-based density check. The model's audio is aligned against the text to see if the phonetic realization matches the diacritic markers.

If a model generates the correct consonant but misses the vowel shift, it is flagged as a Tashkeel Dropout. This prevents "smooth-sounding but linguistically wrong" models from passing.

3

Spectral Variance Check

Using Mel-spectrogram analysis, we measure the boundary between Urdu and Arabic.

A Spectral Variance (ΔS) > 1.5 indicates a robotic jump or glitch. We reject any model that exhibits this, as it destroys the listener's immersion in sacred texts.

4

LFS Finalization

The final verdict is a weighted composite. We prioritize linguistic correctness over mere naturalness.

LFS = (0.4 × TPI) + (0.3 × CSacc) + (0.3 × Naturalness)

Models must exceed the 85% LFS threshold to be considered "Stable".

The Science of DAPA

Why traditional WER is a lie for Urdu and Arabic synthesis.

The "Vowel-Skipping" Problem

In Arabic, a single missing tashkeel (diacritic) can turn a blessing into a curse. Traditional WER checks if the character is there. DAPA checks if the phonetic weight of the diacritic is actually produced in audio.

Nastaliq Ligature Collapse

Urdu's Nastaliq script uses complex vertical stacking. Many neural models "collapse" these into simpler shapes. We measure this via MCD (Mel-Cepstral Distortion) to ensure spectral fidelity.

The Master Equation

+ (0.3 · CSacc)
+ (0.3 · Naturalness)
