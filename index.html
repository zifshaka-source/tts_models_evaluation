Our Unique Approach: DAPA

Most benchmarks are blind to the aural difference between a correct and incorrect vowel in Arabic. We solved this by creating the **Linguistic Fidelity Score (LFS)**.

  • ✓ Tashkeel Preservation Index (TPI): Ensures every diacritic is phonetically represented.
  • ✓ Spectral Continuity: Measures the "glitch-free" transition between Urdu and Arabic.
  • ✓ LFS Integration: A weighted composite that prioritizes linguistic correctness over mere 'smoothness'.
The DAPA Equation
LFS = (0.4 · TPI) + (0.3 · CSacc) + (0.3 · Nat)
