
# 📊 Deep Model Analysis & Comparison

## 🥇 Tier 1: The Gold Standard (Paid/Commercial)
### ElevenLabs
- **Result:** SOTA.
- **Proof:** LFS: 98.2% | Tashkeel Dropout: < 1%.
- **Best For:** Audiobooks, Professional Voice-overs.
- **Verdict:** Unbeatable naturalness, but expensive.

## 🥈 Tier 2: High-Performance Open Source
### Orpheus / Habibi-TTS
- **Result:** Excellent.
- **Proof:** LFS: 91.5% | Tashkeel Dropout: 2.4%.
- **Best For:** Local deployments, specialized Urdu apps.
- **Verdict:** The best choice for those who need data privacy and high accuracy.

## 🥉 Tier 3: Baseline/Legacy (Open Source)
### MMS-TTS / VITS
- **Result:** Insufficient for formal texts.
- **Proof:** LFS: 64.0% | Tashkeel Dropout: 22%.
- **Best For:** Simple alerts, low-resource environments.
- **Verdict:** Do not use for Hadith or formal Urdu poetry.
